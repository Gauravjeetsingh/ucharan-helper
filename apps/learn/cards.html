PRONUNCIATION CARDS

How the ustaad says each word

Each card describes the ustaad's word sound by sound. Practice uses these to decide what to check and how to explain mistakes. They are drafts until you confirm them: listen to the ustaad, fix anything that's wrong, then press Confirm.

  • Check: the app may mark the word red if this sound is different. Tip only: shown as guidance, never marked red (use it when the app can't hear this difference reliably).
  • Voicing (ਭ ਧ ਘ ਝ ਢ): soft = p / t / k / ch / ṭ with tone (nir-pau); voiced = bh / dh / gh / jh / ḍh (bhaag).
